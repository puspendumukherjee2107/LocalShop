# Privacy Policy for LocalStore

**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**App Name:** LocalStore  
**Application ID:** `com.localstore.app`

---

### 1. Introduction
LocalStore ("we", "our", or "the App") is a hyperlocal commerce platform connecting customers with neighborhood merchants and delivery partners. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
When you register or use the LocalStore app, we collect the following information:

1. **Personal Information**:
   * **Name**: To identify your profile and address you during order processing.
   * **Phone Number**: Used as your primary account identifier, for authentication, and for delivery communication.
   * **Delivery Address**: Required for order delivery and calculating merchant distance.
2. **Order & Transaction Data**:
   * Items ordered, quantities, order status, price quotes, and delivery confirmation codes (OTP).
3. **In-App Communications**:
   * Messages sent via in-order live chat between customers, merchants, and delivery drivers.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information
* To process, quote, pack, and fulfill grocery orders.
* To facilitate communication between customer, shopkeeper, and delivery partner for order delivery.
* To verify delivery completion using secure 4-digit OTPs.
* To secure your account and prevent fraudulent logins.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.
* Your delivery address and phone number are shared **only** with the designated merchant and delivery partner fulfilling your specific order.
* We do not integrate third-party ad networks or tracking trackers.

---

### 5. Data Security
* All API communications are transmitted using secure HTTPS encryption.
* Passwords are protected using salted PBKDF2 cryptographic hashing.
* Real-time communications are transmitted securely via encrypted WebSockets.

---

### 6. User Rights & Data Deletion
You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time.  
To request deletion of your account and associated order records, please contact our support team at:
* **Email:** support@localstore.demo (or your registered contact email)
* **Subject:** Account Deletion Request - LocalStore
* Upon receipt of your request, your account and personal identifiers will be permanently removed from the database within 30 days.

---

### 7. Children's Privacy
LocalStore does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 8. Changes to this Policy
We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date.
